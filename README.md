# Call of the Ants

**Version 2.1.1** · [What's new](CHANGELOG.md)

An ant colony simulation that runs in your browser. Colonies lay scent trails, gather food, fight, send out queens to found new nests, and evolve over time as their genes mutate.

**Play:** open the game page in a recent desktop browser (Chrome or Edge work best). The simulation runs on your graphics card through WebGPU; browsers without it get a slower fallback version.

## What you can do

- Start on a random world, load a map from an image, or paint your own map with brushes for temperature, moisture and rarity
- Watch trails form between nests and food, and draw walls to see the ants find a new way
- Follow a single ant, or open a nest to see its genes, its ants and its history
- Speed up time with hyperspeed and watch colonies spread, split into new colours and go to war
- Change almost every rule of the world in the settings
